# IT Access & Security Policy

## Passwords and account lockout
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are rotated every 90 days. After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. To unlock immediately, use the self-service reset at https://sso.company.internal/reset or raise an Access ticket with priority high.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for email, VPN and all SaaS tools behind SSO. Use the Microsoft Authenticator app. If you lose your phone, raise an Access ticket; the IT desk will verify your identity over a video call before resetting MFA.

## VPN
Remote access to internal systems requires the corporate VPN (GlobalProtect). VPN sessions disconnect after 12 hours. If the VPN fails to connect, first restart the GlobalProtect client and check your internet connection, then raise an IT ticket with a screenshot of the error.

## Laptops and software
Company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker / FileVault. Only software from the Company Portal may be installed; any other software needs an IT ticket with manager approval. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported within 24 hours as an urgent IT ticket.

## Access requests
Access to new applications or shared drives is requested through an Access ticket, approved by the reporting manager and the application owner. Standard access is provisioned within 1 business day.
