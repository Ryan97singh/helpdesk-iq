# Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting a claim
Expense claims are submitted in the Expense portal within 30 days of the expense, with original invoices attached. Claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed.

## Approval and payout
Claims are approved by the reporting manager and then verified by Finance. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll if approved before the 20th of the month; otherwise they are paid in the following month's payroll.

## Limits
- Domestic hotel stay: up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 elsewhere.
- Meals while travelling: up to INR 1,200 per day.
- Local travel: cab or ride-hailing receipts are reimbursed at actuals for client visits.
- Internet reimbursement for remote work: up to INR 1,000 per month.

## Non-reimbursable expenses
Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment and upgrades to business class are not reimbursable.
