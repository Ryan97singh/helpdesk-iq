# Hybrid & Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Employees in Gurugram follow a hybrid schedule of 3 days in office (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) and 2 days remote. Teams may agree on different in-office days with their manager's approval.

## Working from another city
Working remotely from another city for more than 2 consecutive weeks requires manager and HR approval in the HR portal. Working from outside India is not permitted without prior approval from HR and Legal.

## Equipment
Employees can request a monitor, keyboard and mouse for home use through an IT ticket. A one-time home-office allowance of INR 5,000 is available after confirmation of employment.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 17:00 IST. Employees should be reachable on Teams during core hours on remote days.
