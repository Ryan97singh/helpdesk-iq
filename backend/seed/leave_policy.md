# Leave Policy

## Annual (earned) leave
Every full-time employee earns 18 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above 10 days lapses on 31 March. Annual leave must be applied for in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance and approved by your reporting manager.

## Sick leave
Employees get 8 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward. For sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work.

## Casual leave
Employees get 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and cannot exceed 3 consecutive days.

## Maternity and paternity leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of fully paid leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Public holidays
The company observes 10 fixed public holidays and 2 optional (restricted) holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published in the HR portal every December.

## Leave without pay
Leave without pay (LWP) is applied automatically when leave balance is exhausted. LWP above 5 days in a quarter requires HR business partner approval.
